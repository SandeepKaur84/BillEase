Features

Everything a small shop needs, nothing it doesn't

Six simple screens that cover the whole day, from opening stock to the last bill.

Product management

Add, edit, search and remove products with their category, price and stock.

Stock tracking

Stock goes down by itself after every sale. Low-stock and out-of-stock items stand out.

Fast billing

Tap products, change quantities, see the total live. You can never sell more than you have.

Printable invoices

Every bill gets an invoice number and a clean page you can print or save as PDF.

Sales history

Every past bill with its date and total. Old invoices keep the price you charged that day.

Live dashboard

Revenue for the week, today's sales, top sellers and what needs restocking, at a glance.

Why BillEase

Built so the numbers are always right

The rules live on the server, not in the browser, so a bill can't be changed on the way and stock can't go wrong, even with two cashiers at once.

  • No oversellingA sale is refused if there isn't enough stock, even if two bills are made at the same moment.
  • Prices from the serverTotals are worked out from the saved price, never from what the browser sends.
  • All or nothingIf one line of a bill fails, nothing is saved and no stock changes.
  • Private by loginOnly signed-in users can see the shop. Passwords are stored as secure hashes.
